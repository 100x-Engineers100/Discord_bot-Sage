Do we have to buy credits to work on Jarvis labs?[j]
A- No, you do not have to buy credits. Please raise a support ticket on discord tagging our 100x team asking for assigning credits to your account and our team will do the needful.. When we dive deep into the curriculum, you will be asked to do a few assignments which may require extra add on credits. Please tag 100x team on the support channel in order to get those credits. 

Best Practices while using Jarvis Labs?
A- Hey! 
1. While using Jarvis Labs you will be asked to make multiple instances, so after practicing please make sure to pause the instance before logging off as the credits will keep depleting from your account if you don't. Use your credits wisely and carefully in order to make the most of it.
2. Keep an eye on your usage report. Jarvis shows GPU-hours and credits spent.
3. Keep a small log (CSV/Notion doc) of what you ran, which instance type, and outcomes. Helps when scaling up or debugging.

Why does the Jarvislabs keep crashing?
A- Hey! It is probably because multiple accounts are trying to hop on the server at the same time. Please wait for a while, clear all the temporary cache and hop back in.
